# Retrieval and Consumption

This area owns the observable retrieval facts that follow from a Customer's Import Approval. A separate `PriceDelivery` domain concept is not established.

## Approved price basis

The permanent business fact is the `ImportApproval` owned by Customer Approval. It identifies the exact immutable Price List Version(s), Price Agreement Version, calculation-rule version, Customer, approval actor/time, and deterministic projection identity needed to reproduce the approved price basis.

The complete projected price rows are derived state. They may be cached for performance but do not need permanent storage as a separate delivery aggregate.

## Exported representations

An exported representation is a deterministic derived result of an `ImportApproval` plus versioned export configuration and export rules.

The generated bytes do not need permanent storage when they can be reproduced deterministically. They may be cached. `DeliveryArtifact` is therefore not modeled as a separate aggregate or permanent business fact.

Historical reproducibility requires the relevant Export Profile/configuration and export-rule version to remain identifiable so that the same approved basis can be regenerated semantically, and where required bitwise, equivalently.

## Price Retrieval

Each successful retrieval is recorded as an immutable `PriceRetrieval` fact. It acts as an append-only log of what PriceSync successfully made available at its boundary.

A Price Retrieval may record provenance such as:

- the referenced `ImportApprovalId`
- export profile/configuration version when applicable
- requested format
- export-rule version
- retrieval timestamp
- retrieving user or integration identity
- deterministic content hash when useful for verification

Repeated successful retrievals create separate Price Retrieval facts. `Available` and `Retrieved` are derived states:

- Available: no successful Price Retrieval exists for the Import Approval
- Retrieved: one or more successful Price Retrieval facts exist

A retrieval fact is created only after export/generation and handoff have succeeded. Failed attempts and retries before successful handoff are operational telemetry rather than domain facts.

## Context relationships

Retrieval consumes the durable approved basis from Customer Approval. Integration Configuration provides versioned Export Profile configuration where an external representation is required. Customer Pricing may rebuild the exact approved projection from the immutable inputs captured by the Import Approval.
