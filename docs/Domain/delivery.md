# Retrieval and Consumption

This area owns the observable retrieval facts that follow from a Customer requesting a valid projection for consumption. A separate `PriceDelivery` or `ImportApproval` domain concept is not established.

## Retrieval basis

A retrieval identifies the exact immutable source versions used to reproduce the projection that was handed off:

- `CustomerId`
- `PriceListVersionId`
- optional `PriceAgreementVersionId`

For current-price retrieval, those versions come from the Customer's Active selections at request time. A Customer may also explicitly request another immutable version combination for historical or exploratory reconstruction.

Retrieval is allowed only when Customer Pricing evaluates the requested basis to a complete valid Customer Price Projection. An invalid projection may be inspected diagnostically but cannot be exported or handed off as Customer Prices.

A requested projection does not need to be current in order to be retrieved. Historical or exploratory valid projections may be exported without changing the Customer's Active source selections.

The complete projected price rows are derived state. They may be cached for performance but do not need permanent storage as a separate delivery aggregate.

## Exported representations

An exported representation is a deterministic derived result of an exact projection basis plus versioned export configuration and export rules.

Projection provenance and representation provenance are separate. A historical projection may therefore be exported through a currently available Export Profile or another explicitly selected compatible Export Profile version. Retrieval records which export configuration was actually used.

The generated bytes do not need permanent storage when they can be reproduced deterministically. They may be cached. `DeliveryArtifact` is therefore not modeled as a separate aggregate or permanent business fact.

Historical reproducibility requires the relevant Export Profile/configuration and export-rule version to remain identifiable so that the same projection basis can be regenerated semantically, and where required bitwise, equivalently.

## Price Retrieval

Each successful retrieval is recorded as an immutable `PriceRetrieval` fact. It acts as an append-only log of exactly what PriceSync successfully handed off at its boundary.

A Price Retrieval may record provenance such as:

- `CustomerId`
- `PriceListVersionId`
- optional `PriceAgreementVersionId`
- export profile/configuration version when applicable
- requested format
- export-rule version
- retrieval timestamp
- retrieving user or integration identity
- deterministic content hash when useful for verification

Repeated successful retrievals create separate Price Retrieval facts. A retrieval fact is created only after export/generation and handoff have succeeded. Failed attempts and retries before successful handoff are operational telemetry rather than domain facts.

Retrieval never changes Price List or Agreement activation state. Whether the retrieved projection was current at request time does not change its projection identity; the exact immutable source provenance records what was consumed.

The transactional boundary is deliberately small: recording a successful handoff must be consistent with that handoff result, but retrieval does not mutate Price Publication, Agreement activation, or Customer Pricing state.

## Context relationships

Retrieval consumes exact immutable projection inputs and uses Customer Pricing to build the corresponding projection. Integration Configuration provides versioned Export Profile configuration where an external representation is required. Current retrieval requests obtain Customer Active selections from Price Publication and Agreements; explicit historical requests may provide specific immutable version references instead.
